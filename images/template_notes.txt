# Template Image Notes

Place your local UI template screenshots in the folders below:

- `images/buttons`
- `images/dialogs`
- `images/anchors`
- `images/reference`

Recommended template names:

- `images/buttons/private_chat_icon.png`
- `images/buttons/upload_image_btn.png`
- `images/buttons/chat_send_btn.png`
- `images/dialogs/creator_page_close_btn.png`
- `images/anchors/creator_page_loaded_anchor.png`
- `images/anchors/chat_window_loaded_anchor.png`

Template screenshots are ignored by Git because they may contain platform UI, account details, or creator data.
